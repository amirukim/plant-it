## Findings

### F-01 — Status badges escape the pot card at narrow widths — R-03 (Hard Gate) — HIGH — **FIXED (approved)**

- **Why**: R-03 requires text never escapes its container. The two metric header rows (`flex … justify-between`, no wrap) held a `whitespace-nowrap` `StatusBadge`; when the metric column is ≤ ~150px the badge spilled past the card edge and was clipped by `.bento-card`'s `overflow: hidden`. Probe reproduced it at 8 of 12 widths (360, 390, 640, 768, 1280, 1366, 1440, 1600) on "Sensor Fault" (Pot 4) and "Optimal" (Pot 3) — worst at 640px (112px columns) and 1280px (113px).
- **Fix applied**: `flex-wrap` on both metric header rows in `src/components/dashboard/PotCard.tsx` (the badge now drops to a second line inside its column; grid columns already had `min-w-0`).
- **Evidence**: card probe re-run at all 12 widths → **0 escapes / 0 clipped**; smoke suite **42/42**; overflow detector clean at 390/820/1280/1440px; build + lint clean.

### F-02 — Em dash character in user-facing strings — R-02 (Hard Gate) — HIGH — open

- **Why**: R-02 forbids the em dash character (`—`) in any text; comma, period, colon or parentheses instead.
- **UI strings (visible in the shipped layout)**:
  1. `src/pages/Dashboard.tsx:80` — "Raspberry Pi Offline — no new sensor data received"
  2. `src/utils/moistureStatus.ts:34` — hint "Too wet — check drainage"
  3. `src/components/dashboard/PotCard.tsx:46` — chip label "Pump —" (empty-value marker)
  4. `src/components/dashboard/PotCard.tsx:211` — "No history yet — sensors not reporting"
  5. `src/components/dashboard/DisplayMode.tsx:18` — chip label "Pump —"
- **In unmounted-but-shipped components**: `AnalyticsCard.tsx:75,146`, `PlantAdvisorCard.tsx:203,226`, 9 strings in `src/data/plantCatalog.ts` (wateringNotes/descriptions).
- **Notes**: code comments are out of UI-copy scope. "Pump —" as an empty marker could become "Pump: no data" / "Pump n/a" — the smoke suite asserts the current label, so the fix updates the test anchor too.

### F-03 — White text on the leaf green fails contrast — R-25 (Hard Gate) — HIGH — open

- **Why**: R-25 requires 4.5:1 for normal-size text (10–12px is not "large text"). White on `--leaf` `#58a65c` = **2.99:1**.
- **Where**:
  1. PotCard "AI" chip — 10px semibold white on `bg-leaf`
  2. PotCard "Pump ON" chip — 12px semibold white on `bg-leaf`
  3. Primary `Button` (white on `--primary #58a65c`): "Try again", Display Mode exit
- **Fix direction**: white text needs a darker ground (white on `--leaf-dark #2f6b3c` = 6.36:1), or keep the green ground and switch to dark text.

### F-04 — Status tone text shades fail 4.5:1 at 12px — R-25 (Hard Gate) — HIGH — open

- **Why**: computed ratios from `toneBadge`/hint usage (backgrounds are 10–12% tints over the white card):
  - `text-warning #b47d20`: **3.12:1** on its badge tint, **3.56:1** on plain white (moisture hint texts)
  - `text-critical #c24e4e`: **4.09:1** on its badge tint (marginal fail; 4.67:1 on white passes)
  - `text-info #4a7fbf`: **≈3.7:1** on its badge tint
  - Pass: healthy 5.66:1, neutral 4.87:1
- **Fix direction**: darken the text tokens only (badges/hints), keep the dot and tint colors; re-verify each pair ≥ 4.5:1.

### F-05 — Display Mode "Pump —" at 70% opacity fails contrast — R-25 (Hard Gate) — HIGH — open

- **Why**: `text-muted-foreground/70` over the card blends to ≈ **3.0:1** at 12px. Full `text-muted-foreground` on white is 5.70:1 — dropping the `/70` fixes it.

Direction *was* given in conversation (palette `#58A65C / #2F6B3C / #E8F5E9 / #F8F6EF`, text `#26352A`, "Duolingo × Apple Health × smart-home", wall-screen use) but exists nowhere in the repo.
